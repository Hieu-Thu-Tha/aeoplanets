What is a 'Prompt Run'?

A Prompt Run is the core unit of value. It means our AI monitoring engine takes one unique question (a query like "best cloud security practices") and automatically runs it across all tracked public AI platforms to check for your citation.

This ensures you are tracking your visibility across the entire AI ecosystem, not just one platform.

Features Explained

Competitive Citation Gap Analysis

This is the most powerful feature. We monitor the questions where the AI cited your competitor but not your website. This provides an immediate, precise action plan to update your content and steal the next citation.

Citation Context Extractor

We don't just show you that you were cited; we extract the exact sentence the AI wrote that used your content. This proves the value of your content and shows you exactly what information the AI trusts.

Top-Cited Content Report

This report reveals your website's best performing pages in the eyes of the AI. Use it as a blueprint to redesign your other pages, ensuring all new content follows the winning structure for maximum AI extractability.
