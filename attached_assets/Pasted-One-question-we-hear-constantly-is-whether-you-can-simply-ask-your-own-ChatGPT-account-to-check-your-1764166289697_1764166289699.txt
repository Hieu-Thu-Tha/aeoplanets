One question we hear constantly is whether you can simply ask your own ChatGPT account to check your AEO position. The short answer is no. Not because the model is inaccurate, but because your own account is not a clean environment. It has been shaped by you, your usage history, your prompts, and your previous conversations. Whether you realise it or not, the model has a localised view of who you are and what your brand does.

This creates a fundamental problem. When you ask your own ChatGPT account to judge your website, it brings all of your past input with it. You have already primed it with your brand name, your messaging, your product details, and even your tone. The result is a distorted perspective that no new visitor or cold AI would ever have. You effectively get an echo of your own narrative, not an impartial assessment.

AEO analysis demands a neutral starting point. The entire point of AEO is to understand what AI thinks of you without context, bias, or prior relationship. This is what a brand new prospect sees when they ask an AI a question about your category. This is what determines who gets recommended, ranked, and repeated.

AEOstars solves this by running your brand through a fresh, isolated environment where no prior memory exists. No past conversations. No saved context. No familiarity with your brand. Just your website, your content, and the AI’s interpretation of it. This is the only way to get a true AEO score and a realistic understanding of how you appear in the moment a cold user asks a question.

Trying to assess your AEO position with your personal ChatGPT account is like asking a long term customer whether your homepage is clear. They already know you. They fill in the gaps. They don’t see the flaws. A brand new buyer, and a clean AI instance, will judge purely on what is in front of them. That is the judgement that matters.

If you want an honest picture of how AI perceives your brand today, you need to strip out personal bias entirely. That is why you should not rely on your own LLM account. And that is why AEOstars exists.
